K-Means Clustering — Customer Segmentation

This application groups customers based on income and spending behavior using the K-Means algorithm.

Problem Description

The objective of this model is to segment customers into groups based on their income and spending habits. This allows businesses to identify patterns and improve decision-making strategies such as marketing and pricing.

Dataset Information

  • Records: 1000 customers
  • Features: Income, Spending
  • Type: Numerical data

Interpretation of Results

The K-Means algorithm successfully grouped customers into three distinct segments:

  • Cluster 1: Low income, low spending
  • Cluster 2: Medium income, moderate spending
  • Cluster 3: High income, high spending

These clusters allow businesses to design targeted strategies for each group, improving customer engagement and maximizing revenue.
